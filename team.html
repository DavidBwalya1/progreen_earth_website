Our Leadership

Pro Green Earth is a Zambian organisation set up to implement the Barotse Rangelands for Restoration Project. We combine deep local knowledge, scientific rigour and long-term partnerships to restore landscapes and improve livelihoods.

Carl Irwin

Founder and former CEO of Zambeef Products PLC, one Southern Africa’s largest agricultural companies and listed on the London AIM and Zambian Stock Exchanges.

Renier Jansen van Vuuren

13+ years of agricultural consulting experience with livestock production. Lead implementation expert for 9 sustainable agriculture and conservation programs in Zambia.

Lucy Pieterse

10+ years of experience in facilitating nature-based solutions and climate finance, leading to verification of c. 12 mtCO2e in reduced emissions in Zambia’s AFOLU sector. 

Christabel Malijani

An expert in environmental health with 40+ years of engagement experience in Zambia’s public and private sectors.
