How the Project Benefits the Community

  • Economic empowerment: Higher incomes from improved livestock and carbon revenue.
  • Food security: Better grazing and animal health for reliable food production.
  • Skills & leadership: Training in sustainable land management and community governance.
  • Equity: Transparent benefit-sharing and inclusive decision-making.
