Our Vision

Transforming Landscapes & Livelihoods

Pro Green Earth is pioneering sustainable land management across Zambia's Western Province, creating lasting environmental and social impact through innovative agricultural practices and community empowerment.

Project Scope

Initial development of 200,000 hectares with potential to scale to 1.2 million hectares of regenerative rangeland in Zambia's Western Province, creating a model for sustainable land use in Africa.

Revenue Streams

Diversified income through carbon credits (VCUs), premium grass-fed beef, sustainable agriculture products, and potential biodiversity credits, ensuring long-term financial viability.

Community Impact

Project value directly benefits communities through improved livelihoods and sustainable practices.

Environmental Impact

Regenerative agriculture and ecosystem restoration at scale, creating carbon sinks and preserving biodiversity.
